Psychology content I created

Three original pieces for college students and the general public. Each is written in plain language and sourced in the references.

1. Infographic: what happens in your body under stress

  1. You assessThe mind judges whether a situation is a threat and whether you can cope with it.
  2. AlarmThe body releases adrenaline. Heart rate and breathing speed up.
  3. FuelCortisol is released, giving the body energy to respond.
  4. RecoveryWhen the pressure passes, the body settles. Rest and slow breathing help it along.

Stress is a normal response. It becomes a concern when it stays high for long periods. Sources: Lazarus & Folkman (1984); American Psychological Association (2018).

3. Short blog post: why encouragement works

When someone tries something hard and we respond with specific, genuine praise, they are more likely to try again. Psychologists call this positive reinforcement: a pleasant consequence that follows a behaviour and makes it more likely to be repeated (Skinner, 1953).

The key is to be specific. “Great job” is nice, but “I noticed you kept going even when it got tricky” tells a person exactly what to repeat. This idea is used in classrooms, therapy and everyday life, and it is one I am learning more about through my coursework and internship.

Ethics, copyright and legal awareness

APA ethics and professional behaviour

The APA Ethics Code asks psychologists to avoid harm, be honest and respect people’s dignity. Online, that means sharing accurate information and not giving diagnoses or personal advice through public posts.

Confidentiality and boundaries

I never share details that could identify a client or child I have observed. I keep a clear line between my personal and professional online presence.

Copyright and Creative Commons

Creative work is protected by copyright automatically. Creative Commons licences tell others how they may reuse it, so I check the licence and follow its terms before using anything.

Image sourcing and attribution

I use my own images or properly licensed ones, and credit the creator, source and licence every time.

Information Technology Act, 2000

India’s main law on electronic records and cyber offences. It includes provisions on identity theft, impersonation and violating someone’s privacy through images. I post only what I have the right to share.

DPDP Act, 2023

India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act focuses on consent and the safe handling of personal data. Its rules were notified in November 2025 and are being phased in. I do not collect or publish anyone else’s personal data here.

How this portfolio applies these principles: no client or case details appear anywhere, all content is my own writing, the graphics were designed for this site, the only photograph is of me, and sources are listed below. This site is for education and is not a substitute for professional help.

Reflection

Building this portfolio showed me that explaining psychology well is a skill of its own. I had to turn ideas like stress responses and reinforcement into short, clear pieces without making them less accurate, and checking each claim against a source became a habit.

I developed skills in layout, visual hierarchy, content planning and referencing, and I became more aware of the ethical and legal side of sharing information online. The main challenges were keeping the design simple while fitting in every section, and keeping each piece short enough to read quickly. Next, I would like to add a short video or audio piece.
